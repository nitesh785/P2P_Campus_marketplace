How it works

Simple for buyers. Simple for sellers.

No payments or shipping inside the app. You find each other here, then meet on campus.

For buyers

  1. 1. Browse

    Open the marketplace to see every available item, newest first.

  2. 2. Search and filter

    Search by keyword and narrow down by category, condition and price.

  3. 3. Contact the seller

    Tap "Message seller" to chat in the app, or contact them on WhatsApp with a message already written.

  4. 4. Meet

    Agree on a public place on campus, like the library or canteen.

  5. 5. Buy

    Check the item, then pay the seller directly by cash or UPI.

For sellers

  1. 1. Create a listing

    Tap "Sell an item" and give it a clear title.

  2. 2. Add details

    Choose a category and condition, set a price and quantity, pick a meeting spot, and add up to three photos.

  3. 3. Publish

    Your listing appears on the marketplace straight away.

  4. 4. Hear from buyers

    Interested students message you in Messages or on WhatsApp.

  5. 5. Complete the exchange

    Meet, get paid, then mark the item as sold from your dashboard. Listings stay up for 60 days and can be renewed.
