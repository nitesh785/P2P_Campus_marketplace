About

Group chats weren't built for buying and selling.

Campus Marketplace gives students one organised place to trade the things campus life runs on: textbooks, calculators, cycles, lab coats and hostel essentials.

The problem

Items were posted in WhatsApp and Telegram groups, where they got buried within hours, couldn't be searched or filtered, and were never marked as sold.

What we built

A campus-only marketplace with search and filters, in-app chat, meeting spots on the campus map, a Wanted board, seller ratings, and an app you can install on your phone.

Why it matters

Seniors pass things on instead of throwing them away, juniors save money, and useful things stay in use on campus.

How students benefit

Made for the way campus trading actually works

Campus Marketplace began as a first-year engineering project. It runs entirely on free services, so it costs nothing to use.

  • Students onlyAccounts are tied to roll numbers on the official student list.
  • Find things fastSearch and filters instead of scrolling through old messages.
  • No feesBuyers pay sellers directly. The platform takes nothing.
  • Close byEvery seller is on your campus, so there's no shipping to arrange.
