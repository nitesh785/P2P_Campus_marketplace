Privacy Policy

Last updated 27 September 2026

Campus Marketplace is a student project. This page explains, in plain words, what we store and who can see it.

What we store

  • Account details: your roll number, full name, email address, WhatsApp number and password. Passwords are stored securely by our authentication provider and are never visible to anyone, including us.
  • Listings: the title, description, price, quantity, category, condition, pickup location, status and photos of items you post.
  • Messages: the text of conversations you have with other students in Messages.
  • Wanted posts, ratings, saved items and reports that you create.

Who can see what

  • Signed-in students can see listings, and the name and WhatsApp number of the student who posted them.
  • Your email address is never shown to other students.
  • Visitors who are not signed in cannot see listings or seller details.
  • Listing photos are stored at hard-to-guess web addresses. Anyone who is given the exact address of a photo can open it, so don't upload photos that show personal information.
  • Messages can be read only by the two students in the conversation.
  • Ratings and wanted posts are visible to signed-in students, with your name. Wanted posts also let students contact you on WhatsApp.
  • Saved items are visible only to you. Reports are visible to you and the admins; the seller isn't told who reported them.
  • Recently viewed items are remembered only in your own browser, not on our servers.
  • The project team (site administrators) can access the database to manage the student list, review reports, and remove content or block accounts that break the rules.

How we use it

Only to run the marketplace: to check that you are a student, show your listings, let buyers contact you, and send account emails such as confirmation and password reset links. We don't sell your data, show ads, or use it for anything else.

Services we use

Data is stored with Supabase (database, login, messages and photo storage). Account emails are sent through Brevo. The website is hosted on Cloudflare Pages, and Cloudflare Web Analytics counts page visits anonymously, without cookies.

Deleting your data

You can delete any of your listings at any time from your Dashboard; this also deletes its photos. To delete your account completely, ask the project team.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
