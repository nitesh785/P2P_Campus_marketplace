By using Campus Marketplace you agree to these terms. They're short because the platform is simple.

Who can use it

Campus Marketplace is for students whose roll number is on the college's official student list. One account per student. Don't register with someone else's roll number or share your account.

What the platform does

We help students find each other to buy and sell items. We are not a party to any sale: we don't handle payments, delivery, returns or refunds. Agreements are between buyer and seller.

Listing rules

  • Describe items honestly, with real photos, and keep the price and status up to date.
  • Mark items as sold or delete them once they're gone. Listings expire after 60 days unless you renew them.
  • Do not list anything illegal, stolen, unsafe or against college rules. This includes weapons, drugs, alcohol, tobacco, counterfeit goods, and exam papers or answer keys.
  • Do not post offensive content or use the platform, including Messages, to harass or spam anyone.
  • Only rate a seller based on a real conversation or deal, and keep reviews honest.
  • Report listings only when you genuinely believe they break these rules.

Meeting and paying

Meet in public places on campus and check items before paying. You are responsible for your own transactions.

Moderation

The project team may remove listings, wanted posts or reviews, and may block accounts that break these rules. Blocked accounts can still browse but can't post or send messages.

No guarantees

Campus Marketplace is a free student project provided as it is. We do our best to keep it running and accurate, but we can't guarantee that it will always be available or error-free.

Changes

We may update these terms. If we do, we'll update this page and the date above.
