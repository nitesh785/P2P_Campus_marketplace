    How it works

    From listing to handshake in four steps

    Everything happens between students. No payments or delivery in the app.

    1. 01

      Find

      Search, filter by category, price or meeting spot, and save items for later.

    2. 02

      Connect

      Tap "Message seller" to chat in the app. Some sellers also share WhatsApp.

    3. 03

      Meet

      Check the meeting spot on the campus map, like the canteen or main gate.

    4. 04

      Done

      Check the item, pay the seller directly, and rate them afterwards.

    Why Campus Marketplace

    Better than scrolling through group chats

    Listings don't get buried, you can search and filter, and sold items disappear on their own.

    Chat in the app

    Message sellers without sharing your number. Unread chats show up right in the menu.

    Meet on the campus map

    Sellers pin a meeting spot, like the Canteen or Boys hostel, so there's no back-and-forth.

    Wanted board

    Can't find it? Post what you need, and students who have it will message you.

    Seller ratings

    After a chat, buyers can rate the seller, so you know who to trust.

    Your number stays private

    WhatsApp is optional. Your number is hidden unless you choose to show it.

    Works like an app

    Install it on your phone for a home-screen icon. Dark mode included.
