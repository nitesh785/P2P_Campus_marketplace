    How it works

    From listing to handshake in four steps

    1. 01

      Find

      Browse or search items listed by students on your campus.

    2. 02

      Connect

      Message the seller in the app or on WhatsApp.

    3. 03

      Meet

      Agree on a time and a public spot on campus.

    4. 04

      Done

      Check the item, pay the seller directly, and they mark it as sold.
