FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can't find your answer? Ask the project team.

What is Campus Marketplace?

A website where students of one campus buy and sell used or new items from each other: books, electronics, furniture, cycles, lab equipment, hostel items and more.

Who can use it?

Only students whose roll number is on the college's official student list. Each roll number can create one account. Listings and seller details are visible only to signed-in students.

How do I sell an item?

Log in, tap "Sell an item", add a title, category, condition, price, description and one to three photos, then publish. It appears on the marketplace right away.

How do I contact a seller?

Open the item and tap "Message seller" to chat inside the website; you'll find your conversations under Messages. If the seller has chosen to share their number, you'll also see "Contact on WhatsApp instead", which opens WhatsApp with a message about the item already written.

Can I edit my listing?

Yes. Go to your Dashboard and tap "Edit" on the listing. You can change any detail and add, remove or replace photos.

How do I delete a listing or mark it as sold?

From your Dashboard. "Mark sold" hides the item from the marketplace (you can relist it later). If you listed more than one, "Sold one" lowers the quantity instead. The delete button removes the listing and its photos permanently.

Is payment handled through the platform?

No. Buyers pay sellers directly, usually by cash or UPI when they meet. Campus Marketplace never handles money and charges no fees.

Where do buyers and sellers meet?

Wherever you both agree on campus. We recommend a public place like the library, canteen or main gate, and checking the item before paying.

Who can see my phone number and email?

Signed-in students can see your name. Your WhatsApp number is private unless you switch on "Show my WhatsApp number" on your Profile; then students can contact you on WhatsApp too. Everyone can always reach you through Messages. Your email is never shown to other students.

Why did my listing disappear from the marketplace?

Listings stay up for 60 days so the marketplace doesn't fill with old items. After that they're marked "Expired" on your Dashboard, where one tap on "Renew" puts them back for another 60 days. Editing a listing also renews it.

What is the Wanted board?

A place to post what you're looking for, like a specific textbook. Students who have it tap "I have this" to message you in the app (or on WhatsApp, if you've chosen to show your number). Mark your request as found when you get it.

How do ratings work?

After a seller has replied to you in Messages, you can rate them from 1 to 5 stars with an optional comment, once per conversation. Ratings appear on the seller's profile with your name.

How do I report a listing?

Open the listing and tap "Report this listing" at the bottom. Choose a reason and add details if you like. Admins from the project team review reports and can remove listings or block accounts. The seller isn't told who reported them.

Can I save items for later?

Yes. Tap the heart on any listing. Your saved items are listed on your Dashboard, and only you can see them.

Can I install it like an app?

Yes. On Android, open the site in Chrome and choose "Install app" or "Add to Home screen" from the menu. On iPhone, open it in Safari, tap Share, then "Add to Home Screen".

I forgot my password. What do I do?

On the login page, enter your email and tap "Forgot password?". We'll email you a link to set a new one.
